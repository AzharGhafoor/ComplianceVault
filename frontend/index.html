NCSA Guidelines Reference

Data Classification Levels (v3.0)

  • C0 (Public): Non-sensitive information.
  • C1 (Internal): Accessible to all employees.
  • C2 (Restricted): Specific teams/departments.
  • C3 (Confidential): Severe damage if leaked.
  • C4 (Top Secret): Grave damage to national interest.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Entities MUST conduct a BIA before selecting controls. The BIA determines whether 'Baseline' or 'Additional' controls apply.

Evidence Retention

Audit evidence should be retained for a minimum period as specified in the organization's data retention policy, aligned with NIA Policy requirements.
